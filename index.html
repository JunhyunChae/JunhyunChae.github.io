Research

Multilayer Propagation of Cross-Country Systemic Risk

Junhyun Chae, Hiroyasu Inoue. Journal of Risk and Financial Management 19(3), 197. March 2026. Code

The model couples cross-border bank lending with trade for 20 economies over 2000–2023. Looking at both layers changes which countries look systemically important. This was my master's research at the University of Hyogo with Prof. Hiroyasu Inoue.
