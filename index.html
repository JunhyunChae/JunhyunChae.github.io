About

I'm Junhyun Chae, a blockchain engineer at InterTrade in Tokyo. I build digital asset custody for financial institutions and present it to them, from proof of concept to delivery. Recent work: a PKCS#11 signing server that signs with keys held in the customer's HSM, and pre-signing checks for token issuance. Before this, I researched how financial shocks travel between countries.

Korean is my first language, Japanese is my working language, and English is how I work with vendors overseas. I grew up in Seoul, did my master's in Kobe, and now work in Tokyo.
